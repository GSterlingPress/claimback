# Compliance constitution

ClaimBack is claims workflow software, not a mechanism to manufacture entitlement.

Never impersonate a seller, collect Walmart passwords, bypass authorization, invent evidence/losses/amounts, submit unsupported claim types, call potential recovery guaranteed, or enable production before Walmart approval.

Required: authorized seller + documented shipment + deterministic eligibility + real evidence + audit event.

Walmart approval does not replace legal review of our terms, privacy, document handling, marketing, or compensation.
